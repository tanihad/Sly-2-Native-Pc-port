Real-ESRGAN x4plus (models\x4plus.bin)
--------------------------------------

The "Reimagined" look sharpens the game's textures on your PC with the Real-ESRGAN
x4plus network. models\x4plus.bin holds its weights, converted from the published
model file RealESRGAN_x4plus.pth into the format this game reads; nothing else was
changed. Real-ESRGAN is by Xintao Wang and contributors:
https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN
